Venue

Conference location and travel information

The National School of Applied Sciences - El Jadida (ENSAJ)

The 9th International Conference on Industrial and Logistics Systems (SIL'2026) will be hosted at the National School of Applied Sciences of El Jadida (ENSAJ).

Established in 2008 and affiliated with Chouaib Doukkali University, ENSAJ is a prestigious public engineering school in Morocco. The institution is dedicated to training state-certified engineers, focusing on innovation, professional training, and advanced scientific research in various fields including Computer Engineering, Telecommunications, Energy Engineering, and Industrial Engineering.

Location

ENSAJ is located in the beautiful coastal city of El Jadida, in the El Haouzia area.
